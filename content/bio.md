Angelika Erne is a Zurich-based mixed media artist whose work weaves vibrant textures, colors, and raw emotion into compelling visual stories. Drawing inspiration from the dynamics of everyday life and personal expression, she explores multi-layered techniques that bridge modern abstract styles with tactile, expressive depth.

Beyond her studio practice, Angelika brings art to life as an Art Instructor at PaintEvents. Whether hosting dynamic group workshops or curating intimate private painting events, she is passionate about helping others unlock their creative spark, discover new techniques, and experience the joy of making art in an inclusive, inspiring setting.
